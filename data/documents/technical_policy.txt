
Technical and IT Policy

All employees must use company-approved software for official work.

Employees must use multi-factor authentication when accessing company systems.

Company laptops must have antivirus protection enabled.

Employees must not share their account passwords with anyone.

All technical support requests must be submitted through the IT helpdesk.

Critical system incidents must be reported immediately to the IT department.

Access to confidential company data requires authorization from the department manager.
